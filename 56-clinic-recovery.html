FEATURED PROJECT

Recovering 56 Healthcare Clinics

A large-scale production recovery involving dozens of electronic medical record databases, coordinated technical teams, and restoring critical healthcare systems safely and consistently.

Read the Story Back to Projects

The Situation

The phone call that changed my vacation.

I was on vacation when I received a phone call from work. A software deployment had gone wrong, resulting in a production database overwrite that affected dozens of healthcare clinics. Electronic Medical Record systems were unavailable, and a large, coordinated recovery effort was beginning.

I immediately shifted from vacation mode into incident mode. The priority wasn't simply restoring databases—it was helping restore systems that physicians, nurses, and clinic staff relied on every day to care for patients.

As more information became available, it was clear this would be one of the largest recovery efforts of my career.

The Challenge

Recover safely. Recover consistently.

Recovering one production database requires care and validation. Recovering dozens requires planning, coordination, and constant communication.

Every database needed to be restored correctly, validated before being returned to service, and tracked alongside the work of infrastructure, application, and support teams. The challenge wasn't only technical—it was organizational.

My Role

Bringing structure to a complex recovery.

I joined the coordinated recovery effort, working with database, infrastructure, application, and support teams to prioritize clinic restores, validate recoveries, and keep everyone aligned as the incident evolved.

Throughout the recovery, I focused on remaining calm, communicating clearly, and making sure each clinic was restored safely before moving on to the next. In situations like these, consistency is just as important as speed.

Outcome

Recovering service while improving future processes.

The affected clinics were successfully recovered and patient data was protected. The incident also reinforced the importance of recovery planning, documentation, communication, and continuous improvement. Some of the most valuable operational improvements come from learning after difficult days.

Lessons Learned

Every major incident leaves behind something valuable.

Preparation Matters

Recovery begins long before an outage occurs.

Communication Builds Confidence

Keeping teams informed reduces confusion and allows technical experts to focus on solving problems.

Stay Calm

The best decisions during production incidents come from clear, methodical thinking—not panic.
