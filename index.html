Working Together

Technical expertise matters, but the way we work together matters just as much. These are the principles I bring to every team, project, and production incident.

Calm Under Pressure

Production incidents demand clear thinking. I focus on understanding the problem, organizing the response, and keeping communication steady while the team works toward recovery.

Solving today's problem is important. Understanding why it happened and improving the process so it is less likely to happen again is even more valuable.

About Me

Database expertise backed by operational leadership.

I believe the best database administrators do more than keep systems online—they create confidence.
